*mini-surround-highlight.txt*  Fork additions: highlight on actions

The `sa`, `sd`, and `sr` actions briefly highlight the affected text.

                                               *MiniSurround-fork-hl-groups*
- `MiniSurroundAdd` - temporary highlighting of added surrounding.
- `MiniSurroundDelete` - temporary highlighting after deleted surrounding.
- `MiniSurroundReplace` - temporary highlighting of replaced surrounding.

Each group links to a Diff group by default. To change one, set it directly
with |nvim_set_hl()|.
